# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in `crawler-labyrinth` a harassment-free, welcoming, and technically constructive experience for everyone regardless of background, experience level, or identity.

## Expected Behavior

- Focus reviews and discussions on reproducible behavior, security, accessibility, and code clarity.
- Communicate respectfully and assume good intent when reviewing issues and pull requests.
- Give credit accurately and accept constructive feedback gracefully.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks, or public/private intimidation.
- Publishing others' private information or security exploits prior to coordinated disclosure.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may close threads, remove abusive comments, or restrict repository participation for violations of this Code of Conduct. Reports can be raised via [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/arnoldwender/crawler-labyrinth-js/issues) or [arnoldwender.com](https://arnoldwender.com).
